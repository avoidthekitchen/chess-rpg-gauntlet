## Status and promise

Implementation baseline, 2026-10-24. Regions 1–5 and the Lich King finale are registered in the complete review build.
The owner approved completing the published-puzzle journey. The chapter briefs record source fit and validation; the release record distinguishes built content from pending checks.

Players must already know how pieces move. The core teaches tactical recognition, calculation, defensive resources, and short endgame decisions.
It does not yet promise complete opening play, sustained endgame technique, or competence in full games.
Keep the five factions, companions, chapter turns, and the Lich King's refusal to accept a loss.
Use chapter reviews as story confrontations. Hired armies, powers, faction AI, and live army bosses remain deferred.

## Teaching pattern

- Teach one mechanism clearly, then vary the moving piece, board shape, and opponent's reply.
- Before choosing a move, check the opponent's threats and your king's safety. Calculate their strongest reply.
- Teach defence explicitly. A winning attack against a pinned enemy does not teach escaping your own pin.
- Revisit an idea after intervening lessons, then in a later Region. The path supplies spacing without a scheduling service.
- Make demonstrations optional. Keep retries available and stars optional, under the existing assistance rules.
- Assess with fresh, mixed positions. Neutral titles, prompts, portraits, and banners must not reveal the motif before the move.
- Offer repeated sets as optional practice. Value correct calculation before speed; never make a timer or repeated set a progression gate.

These are our adaptations, not a claim of course equivalence or proven rating gains:

The finale ends with the Lich King accepting the actual checkmate on its final board. His refusal to lose is the story's conflict.
The player succeeds by learning and retrying. Gauntlet work remains separate from completing this core journey.

For Region 4, a developing move must improve an undeveloped piece while creating the puzzle's forcing threat.
An opening tag alone does not qualify. File tactics illustrate use of a file, not a complete strategy for controlling one.
Defence must answer a threat visible in the displayed position. Do not reverse an attacking puzzle or invent a reply.
For Region 5, inspect pawn arrival times, king legality, rook purpose, and promotion choice on the actual line.
A queen promotion must fail where a lesson claims underpromotion is required; do not infer necessity from the tag.
If a planned slot lacks suitable published sources, record the gap and narrow the claim or replace the slot with a supported skill.
Never fill a content gap by composing a puzzle, changing its pieces, or extending its published endpoint.

## Content and release acceptance

- Every required node has a focused objective, prerequisites, one demonstration, playable waves, useful hints, and backups.
- New ordinary lessons start with three waves. Add a wave only when it teaches a distinct necessary variation.
- Demonstrations, practice, backups, and first assessment attempts use distinct source IDs across the journey.
- Replaying known positions is labeled practice. Retried assessments are supported but are no longer described as unseen tests.
- Record source ID, URL, original FEN and moves, rating, quality fields, teaching reason, and each mechanism's decisive move.
- Keep existing quality filters and legal replays. Check source equality, lesson fit, duplicate reservations, and nearby backup difficulty.
- Neutral review text preserves recognition. Requested hints may reveal the moving piece under the existing assistance rules.
- Completion opens the next chapter. Clean and no-hint stars remain optional and can be earned later.
- New rules require unit tests. Preserve R1/R2 saves, stars, revisits, and separate legacy saves.
- Review teaching claims independently. Exercise complete navigation, retries, reloads, exits during replies, and all playbook viewport sizes.
- Run `npm run ci` for the implementation PR and `npm run ci -- --full` before shipping a Region. Record actual results in build progress.
- Keep unbuilt chapters visibly unavailable. Do not describe proposed content or missing technique capabilities as shipped.

Region 4 does not claim overload or a complete opening course. Development starts from a bishop’s home square; unseen game history is not assumed.

Region 5 teaches short endgame decisions. Knight promotion is required for immediate mate, without claiming that every queen promotion loses.
Opposition, key squares, Lucena, Philidor and sustained conversion remain separate technique work.
